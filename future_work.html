Frequently Asked Questions
What is Form Fill Up? +
Form Fill Up refers to registering a new user using their authentic details and your 6-digit referral UID code.
When will I receive 170 Taka? +
You receive 170 Taka immediately after the Main Admin approves and activates the referred user's account.
How does Account Activation work? +
Users complete the 520 Taka activation fee payment. Admin verifies payment details and sets status to Active.
Minimum Withdraw & Interval? +
Minimum withdrawal is 100 Taka. Withdrawals are permitted once every 3 days (72 hours) with at least 1 Active Referral.
Important Rules & Anti-Fraud Notice
  • Never create fake or duplicate accounts on the same device.
  • Always provide real, working WhatsApp numbers and emails.
  • Do not spam or mislead new users.
  • Violating platform policies will result in account ban.
